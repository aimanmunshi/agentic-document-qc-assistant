PAYER LANDSCAPE ASSESSMENT
Client: Ferngate Biosciences
Prepared by: Analytics & Insights Team
Date: 06/02/2025

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This assessment provides an overview of the current payer landscape relevant
to the upcoming launch of Ferngate's lead asset. It summarizes coverage
policy trends across the top twenty commercial payers by covered lives and
highlights areas of anticipated friction during the first twelve months
post-launch.

2. PAYER COVERAGE OVERVIEW
Of the twenty payers assessed, twelve currently indicate a favorable
predisposition toward coverage without restriction, five indicate likely
prior-authorization requirements, and three remain undetermined pending
internal formulary committee review. Combined, the twelve favorable payers
represent approximately 61% of covered commercial lives in the assessed
set.

2.1 Regional Variation
Favorable coverage sentiment was strongest among payers headquartered in the
Northeast and Mid-Atlantic regions. Payers in the South Central region were
more likely to indicate an intent to impose step-therapy requirements ahead
of approving the asset without restriction.

3. COMPETITIVE CONTEXT
Two competing assets in the same therapeutic class have already secured
formulary placement with a subset of the assessed payers. Both competitors
carry list prices within 10% of Ferngate's proposed list price, suggesting
that coverage decisions in this class are likely to be driven more by
clinical differentiation than by price alone.

4. FINDINGS AND IMPLICATIONS
The overall payer sentiment is more favorable than the category average
observed in prior Ferngate launches. However, the three undetermined payers
account for a meaningful share of covered lives in the West region
specifically, and the outcome of their formulary committee reviews should
be treated as a key swing factor for early launch performance in that
region.

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
We recommend Ferngate prioritize payer account management outreach toward
the three undetermined payers ahead of their formulary committee reviews,
and prepare step-therapy appeal materials in advance for the South Central
region given the elevated likelihood of restriction there.

END OF REPORT
