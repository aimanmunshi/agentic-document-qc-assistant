MARKET ACCESS SUMMARY REPORT
Client: Northbridge Pharma Solutions
Prepared by: Analytics & Insights Team
Date: 03/14/2025

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report summarizes reimbursement performance for the Q1 2025 product
launch across the three priority regions: Northeast, Midwest, and West Coast.
Overall uptake has tracked slightly ahead of the base-case forecast, driven
primarily by favorable formulary placement in the Northeast region.

2. METHODOLOGY
Data was aggregated from payer claims feeds and reconciled against the
client's internal CRM records on a monthly basis. Figures below reflect
confirmed, adjudicated claims only; pending claims are excluded.

3. REGIONAL PERFORMANCE

The table below shows confirmed unit sales by region for the reporting
period, along with associated net revenue.

Region          Units Sold      Net Revenue (USD)
Northeast       12,450          1,867,500
Midwest         8,900           1,157,000
West Coast      6,320           948,000

Total confirmed units across all regions for the period: 27,670
Total net revenue for the period: $4,205,500

3.1 Commentary
Northeast continues to outperform relative to the original launch model,
while Midwest tracked roughly in line with expectations. West Coast lagged
slightly behind forecast, largely attributable to a delayed formulary
decision by a key regional payer that was only resolved in the final month
of the quarter.

4. RISK FACTORS
- Continued formulary volatility in the West Coast region
- Potential rebate renegotiation with the largest Midwest payer in Q2
- Upcoming generic entrant expected in late 2025, timeline still uncertain

5. RECOMMENDATIONS
We recommend the client prioritize payer engagement resources toward the
West Coast region ahead of the next contracting cycle, and continue
monitoring the Midwest rebate discussion closely given its outsized
contribution to net revenue.

END OF REPORT
