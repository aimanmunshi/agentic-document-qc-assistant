QUARTERLY ANALYTICS REVIEW
Client: Alderstone Health Group
Prepared by: Analytics & Insights Team
Date: May 12, 2025

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This document reviews performance for Q1 across the Alderstone specialty
portfolio. Data collection for the period began on 01/02/2025 and closed on
March 31, 2025. All figures below are final and reconciled.

2. METHODOLOGY
Claims data was pulled weekly from the payer clearinghouse starting
1/2/2025. A mid-quarter checkpoint review was conducted on 15/02/2025 to
validate early trends, with the final data freeze occurring on
2025-03-31.

3. PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW
Quarter 1 performance exceeded internal projections across all three
tracked brands. The Q1 growth rate of 14.2% compares favorably to the prior
quarter's growth rate of 9.8%. This marks the strongest start to a calendar
year quarter that the portfolio has seen since early 2023.

3.1 Brand-Level Detail
Brand A showed the most pronounced gains during Quarter 1, with new patient
starts up 22% versus the same period last year. Brand B and Brand C posted
more modest gains of 6% and 4% respectively across the Q1 window.

4. UPCOMING MILESTONES
- Mid-year business review scheduled for 07/15/2025
- Brand C label update expected around August 3, 2025
- Next quarterly checkpoint call: 10/01/2025

5. RISK FACTORS
Continued monitoring is recommended heading into next quarter given some
early softness observed in the Brand C trend line during the back half of
Q1. A follow-up deep-dive is planned once Quarter 1 numbers are fully
audited.

6. RECOMMENDATIONS
We recommend maintaining current field deployment levels through the next
quarter and revisiting the Brand C softness at the mid-year business
review on 15 July 2025.

END OF REPORT
